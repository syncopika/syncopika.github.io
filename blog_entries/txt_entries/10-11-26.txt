--p
I made another solo trip to Taiwan (Tainan and Taipei this time) and Naha, Okinawa for the first time from 10/02 - 10/09! :D I flew EVA Air (787-9) on their new direct route from IAD <-> TPE and Starlux from TPE <-> OKA. From TPE -> OKA, I flew an A350-900 and on the way back an A330neo.

I went to Okinawa first and then Taiwan. I got super lucky with the weather in both places and only saw rain once I think (briefly too, when I was in the airport to leave Naha).

--h2
Tainan, Taiwan (台南)
--p
I stayed near the Tainan TRA station, which is a great location and fairly close to a lot of historic sights to see in downtown Tainan.

--h3
Military aircraft! :O
--p
Was pleasantly surprised at the constant jet noise in Tainan :). Everyday there were C-130s and AIDC F-CK-1s flying around so jet noise is a common occurrence. The fighters were frequently flying in pairs :O.
